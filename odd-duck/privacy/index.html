Odd Duck — Privacy Policy

Paper Lantern Games · Effective 14 October 2026

In short: we collect no personal data ourselves: no accounts, no analytics. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Your game progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Odd Duck is a duck-spotting puzzle game made by Paper Lantern Games. This policy covers the Odd Duck app for Android distributed on Google Play.

What the app stores

To remember your game, the app saves the following only on your device: the stars you earned on each level, your hints, your Daily Flock streak and best times, and your settings (sound and reduced motion). None of this identifies you, and none of it leaves your device.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes this data. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup under Google's own terms; we never receive it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a full-screen ad after some levels (at most one every minute) and offers optional "watch a video" rewards for extra hints. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process: your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ad interactions and app usage, and diagnostic data. Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad choices in the game. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Without a connection no ads are shown and the game plays on.

Ads are rated for teens at most; the app does not target children.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal data ourselves, and we receive no data that identifies you.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services of our own.
  • We do not ask for any permissions to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • There are no accounts, purchases or chat.

Children

Odd Duck is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Other versions of the game

Odd Duck can also be played in web browsers on game portals. Those versions are hosted by the portal, and the portal's own privacy policy applies to them.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes what it collects, we will update this page and the app's Google Play data-safety section before that version is released. The first release (versionCode 1) had no ads and collected nothing.
