Crust Crusher — Privacy Policy

Paper Lantern Games · Effective 14 October 2026

In short: the Crust Crusher app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Crust Crusher is a planet-smashing game made by Paper Lantern Games. This policy covers the Crust Crusher app for Android distributed on Google Play.

What the app stores

To remember your game, the app saves the following only on your device: the state of your galaxy: your gems, upgrades, planets, moonlets and stardust, your daily-planet streak, and your settings (music, sounds, quality). None of this identifies you, and none of it leaves your device.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes this data. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup under Google's own terms; we never receive it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal or device data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We do not ask for any permissions to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • There are no accounts, purchases or chat.

Children

Crust Crusher is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Other versions of the game

Crust Crusher can also be played in web browsers on game portals. Those versions are hosted by the portal, and the portal's own privacy policy applies to them.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes what it collects (for example, by adding optional ads), we will update this page and the app's Google Play data-safety section before that version is released.
