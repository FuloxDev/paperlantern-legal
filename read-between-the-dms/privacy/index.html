Privacy Policy

Read Between the DMs · last updated 17 October 2026

Read Between the DMs is a card game. There is no account and no sign-in, and the game runs no server of its own: we receive nothing about you. Your name, avatar, XP and settings stay on your device. The Android app shows an ad between matches, served by Google AdMob, and the version on Playgama shows ads from Playgama. Those ad companies collect some information to do that (see "Advertising" below). This policy explains what the game keeps on your device, what is shared with other players if you play nearby, and what the ad companies collect.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved in your browser's or the app's local storage. It is never sent to us. The "Playing nearby" section below explains what is shared with the other phones at a table you join:

WhatWhy
Display name you typeSo you don't retype it each time
Chosen avatarTo remember your pick
XP and levelProgression across matches
Sound, music and speed settingsTo remember your preferences
A randomly generated device idTo reconnect you to a nearby game in progress if you drop out

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → Read Between the DMs → Storage → Clear data) or your browser's site data. Uninstalling the app also removes it from the device.

Android backup. The Android app uses the standard Android Auto Backup, so the data above may be copied to your own Google Drive and restored if you reinstall the app. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms — we cannot see it, and it is never sent to us. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup, or per-app in your device's backup settings.

Playing nearby

Solo play against computer opponents sends no game data anywhere. In the Android app you can also host a table on your phone, or join a friend's, over your local network. When you do, your display name, your avatar, your chat messages and your moves are sent directly between the phones at that table, over your Wi-Fi or hotspot. They never reach us.

WhatWhy
The display name you typed, and your avatarSo other players at the table can see who is who
The randomly generated device idTo reconnect you to your seat if your connection drops
Your moves and chat messagesTo play the game and show them to the other players at the table

The connection between phones is not encrypted, so play on networks you trust. A phone that is looking for tables listens for other phones' announcements on the local network. The table lives on the host's phone and is gone when the host closes it; we do not receive a copy and we do not build profiles.

The display name is whatever you type. It does not have to be your real name, and we would rather it wasn't. Please don't type anything into the name or chat that you would mind another player seeing.

Advertising

Android app (Google AdMob). The app shows a full-screen ad between some matches. It never shows one after your first match, and never more than one every few minutes. To pick and measure ads and to stop fraud, AdMob collects:

WhatWhy
Your device's advertising IDAd delivery, frequency limits and measurement
IP address (approximate location)Choosing ads for your region, fraud prevention
Device and app details (model, Android version, app version)Showing an ad that works on your device
Ad interactions and diagnosticsMeasurement and fixing ad problems

This data goes to Google, not to us, and Google handles it under its policy for partner apps. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

Your consent. Where the law asks for it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app shows Google's consent form (made with Google's UMP tool) the first time you open it, and no ad is requested until you have answered. You can change your answer at any time: open Settings and tap ad privacy. Where no consent form is needed, that button brings you to this section instead. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Web version on Playgama. If you play on Playgama, the game shows an ad between matches through Playgama's ad system. Playgama and its ad partners decide what is collected, ask for consent and handle your data under Playgama's own privacy policy and cookie choices. We do not receive that data. The same goes for any other site that hosts the game and shows its own ads.

No ads: the itch.io version and the CrazyGames version of the game show no ads from us.

What we do not do

  • No server of our own. The game sends nothing to us, and we hold no data about you.
  • No accounts, sign-in or passwords, and no email addresses collected.
  • No ad network of our own: ads come only from Google AdMob (Android app) and Playgama (web version on Playgama).
  • No analytics and no tracking cookies of our own.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. We do not ask for anyone's age, and the game sends us nothing, so there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

Your choices

  • Play solo on itch.io and nothing is transmitted at all.
  • Change your ad consent from Settings → ad privacy, or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Play nearby only on networks you trust.
  • Clear the app's data to erase everything stored on your device.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be noted here before they take effect.
